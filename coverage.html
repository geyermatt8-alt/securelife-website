Coverage options

Understand your choices

A quick, plain-language overview of common types of life insurance so you can have a more informed conversation with a licensed agent.

Popular

Term life insurance

Coverage for a set period — often 10, 20, or 30 years. Typically the most affordable way to get a larger amount of coverage during the years your family depends on your income.

Lifelong

Whole life insurance

Permanent coverage that lasts your whole life and can build cash value over time. Premiums are generally higher but stay level.

Flexible

Universal life insurance

Permanent coverage with flexible premiums and a cash-value component, offering more adjustability as your needs change.

Simplified

Final expense insurance

Smaller whole-life policies designed to help cover end-of-life costs such as funeral and burial expenses, often with a simplified application.

Please note: This information is general and educational only and is not insurance advice or a recommendation. Availability, terms, and pricing vary. A licensed insurance professional can help you compare options for your specific situation.
