SecureLife Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the SecureLife website or submit a request for information, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your first name, last name, email address, telephone number, ZIP code, age range, desired life insurance coverage amount, and information about whether you currently have life insurance.

We may also collect information about how you interact with our website, including information about the advertising source or campaign that directed you to our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to respond to your request and help connect you with life insurance agents, agencies, insurance professionals, or other providers that may be able to assist you with life insurance options.

We may also use information to operate and improve our website, measure advertising performance, communicate with you regarding your request, prevent fraud, maintain business records, and comply with applicable law.

3. Sharing of Information

SecureLife may share information you submit with third parties, including insurance agencies, insurance agents, insurance professionals, carriers, service providers, and business partners, for purposes related to responding to your request for life insurance information or providing related services.

Depending on how your request is handled, your information may be provided to one or more insurance professionals or agencies that may contact you regarding life insurance products or services.

4. Communications

If you provide your contact information and applicable consent, you may be contacted regarding life insurance products or services.

Communications may occur by telephone, email, or text message, subject to applicable law and the permissions and consent you provide.

Consent to receive marketing communications is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

5. Advertising and Tracking

SecureLife may use cookies, URL parameters, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to understand how visitors arrive at our website, measure advertising performance, improve our website, and understand how users interact with our services.

Advertising platforms and analytics providers may collect information about website visits and interactions in accordance with their own privacy policies.

6. Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to operate our website, store information, process data, analyze website traffic, provide advertising services, maintain technical infrastructure, and perform other business functions.

These providers may have access to information as reasonably necessary to perform services on our behalf.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes, including responding to requests, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, preventing fraud, complying with legal obligations, and operating our business.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain information.

Requests may be subject to verification and applicable legal exceptions.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. SecureLife is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before providing personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

SecureLife is not directed toward children under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how SecureLife handles personal information, please contact us using the contact information provided on our website.
