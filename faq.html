FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often. Still curious? Just reach out.

Is requesting a quote really free?

Yes. Requesting information and quotes through SecureLife is completely free, and there's no obligation to purchase anything.

Do I need a medical exam to get started?

No exam is required to request a quote or information. Depending on the product and provider, some policies may require a medical exam later in the application process, while others may not — a licensed agent can explain the specifics.

Who will contact me after I submit the form?

A licensed insurance agent or agency may contact you to discuss options. You choose to be contacted when you submit the form, and consent is never a condition of buying anything.

How is my personal information used?

Your information is used to help respond to your request and connect you with licensed insurance professionals. It is transmitted over a secure, encrypted connection. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How much coverage do I need?

It depends on your income, debts, family situation, and goals. Many people consider coverage that would help replace income and cover major obligations. A licensed agent can help you estimate an amount that fits your needs.

Can I opt out of communications?

Yes. You can opt out of text messages by replying STOP, and you can ask not to be contacted at any time.

The information on this page is general and educational only and is not insurance advice. Product availability and terms vary by provider and state.
