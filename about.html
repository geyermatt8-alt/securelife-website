About us

Helping families take the first step

SecureLife makes it simple to explore life insurance options and connect with licensed professionals — on your terms.

Our mission

Life insurance can feel complicated. Our goal is to make the first step easy: a short, no-pressure form that helps you understand your options and connect with licensed insurance professionals who can answer your questions.

What we do

SecureLife is a free service that helps consumers request information about life insurance. When you submit a request, we help connect you with licensed insurance agents or agencies that may contact you to discuss coverage options that fit your needs.

  • A quick, straightforward way to request quotes and information.
  • Connections to licensed insurance professionals.
  • No cost and no obligation to purchase anything.

Our commitment to your privacy

We take your privacy seriously. Your information is transmitted over a secure, encrypted connection and is used to help respond to your request. You decide whether to be contacted, and consent is never a condition of purchasing any product or service. You can review the details anytime in our Privacy Policy.

Disclosure: SecureLife is not an insurance company, agency, or provider and does not issue policies or give insurance advice. We help connect consumers with licensed insurance professionals who may offer products and services.
